FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you cover all of Bromley?
Yes. We take on commercial work across the London Borough of Bromley, including Bromley town centre, Beckenham, Orpington, Chislehurst, West Wickham, Keston, Petts Wood and Biggin Hill.
You are based in Kent. Is Bromley outside your area?
No. Bromley is within our working area, alongside west Kent towns such as Sevenoaks, Swanley and Dartford.
Are you accredited and insured?
Yes. Roundwood Solutions is BALI-accredited and carries £5 million public liability insurance. All herbicide work is carried out by PA1 and PA6 certified operatives.
Can you take over from our current grounds contractor?
Yes. We survey the site, review your current specification and quote against it, so you can compare like for like.
Do you charge for a site survey?
No. Site surveys and quotes are free and there is no obligation.
